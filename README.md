# EPA TSCA Inventory RDF

Complete July 2025 EPA TSCA Inventory transformation. CAS-identified
substances and confidential PMN accession records remain distinct regulatory
entries. Parseable structures link directly to canonical InChIKey compound
hubs; unresolvable structures retain their EPA/CAS identity without guessing.

The EPA source is a U.S. government work. PubChem/CompTox-derived structures
are retained with their provenance.
